# Phase 5.42 – Chapter 7: How do Organisms Reproduce?

**Revision:** v2.9.91  
**Baseline:** v2.9.90  
**Review date:** 8 November 2026

## Scope

Reviewed all 60 active questions across three 20-question sets. Checked each question stem, four options, stored answer index, selected answer and explanation. The active set files are the three `*_set1.json`, `*_set2.json` and `*_set3.json` files under `data/in/cbse10/science/`.

## Results

- 60/60 question IDs matched the expected active records.
- 60/60 answer indexes valid; 60/60 explanations present and checked.
- 38 question records retained without content changes.
- 22 question records amended for scientific accuracy, wording precision or removal of overstatement.
- All stored answer keys were retained after independent checking; the Leishmania Assertion–Reason item was clarified to match the NCERT description of a definite longitudinal orientation relative to the flagellum.

## Material corrections

1. Clarified that condoms reduce STI risk but do not provide complete protection against every STI.
2. Removed the inaccurate requirement that Plasmodium multiple fission occurs inside a protective cyst.
3. Reworded menstruation to avoid treating 28 days as universal or implying that the ovum is necessarily visibly shed with menstrual flow.
4. Corrected Copper-T explanations to focus on copper’s effects on sperm movement/function and reduced fertilisation; removed claims that implantation prevention is its primary mechanism.
5. Removed a fixed semen pH claim and retained a qualified explanation of seminal secretions.
6. Corrected the PCPNDT Act explanation: fetal sex determination/selection and disclosure are prohibited; legitimate prenatal diagnostic procedures are regulated and remain permitted for specified medical indications.
7. Removed wheat as a typical wind-pollinated example; retained maize.
8. Corrected the Leishmania Assertion–Reason answer key: the “strictly along the flagellum axis” assertion is false, while the definite orientation in relation to the flagellum is true.
9. Qualified claims about cryptorchidism, ectopic pregnancy, missed contraceptive pills, grafting and fertility after IUD removal.
10. Corrected the claim that penicillin reliably cures gonorrhoea; appropriate antibiotic selection must reflect resistance and current clinical guidance, while HIV requires antiretroviral therapy.
