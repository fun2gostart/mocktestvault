AUTHENTICATION STANDARD (REQUIRED BEFORE ANY PYQ CLAIM)
1. Original question paper or candidate response-sheet export, not a generic syllabus/curriculum URL.
2. Exact exam, year, subject, paper/set/code, shift and question number.
3. Exact stem and option correspondence, allowing only documented formatting normalization.
4. Official final answer key / marking scheme and accepted answer mapping.
5. Reviewer, review date, source page/question reference, and disposition for ambiguous or changed items.

DATASET-LEVEL DISPOSITION
- CBSE year-labelled datasets: independent year-labelled practice drills; not authenticated reproductions of CBSE annual board papers.
- SSC shift-labelled datasets: independent shift-labelled practice compilations; not authenticated reproductions of the SSC CGL Tier-I 2023 shift papers.
- Keep current practice content available only with the existing disclaimers.
- Do not remove practice pages solely because original-paper provenance is unavailable.
- Do not use “official PYQ”, “actual previous-year paper”, or equivalent claims until item-level evidence is matched.
