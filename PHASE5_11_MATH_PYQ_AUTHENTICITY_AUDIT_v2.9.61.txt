PROVENANCE FINDINGS
1. The supplied datasets contain a year-labelled exam field and legacy notes referring to official CBSE examinations, but no item-level original-paper URL, set/code, question number, or official marking-scheme match was supplied.
2. Each record's prior citation explicitly described the content as a year-labelled practice set and said the exact original paper/key match was not authenticated.
3. The linked MockTestVault HTML pages already disclose that they are independent practice sets and that original board-paper/official marking-scheme matching has not been completed. This page-level disclosure was retained.
4. A CBSE archive landing page is a discovery route, not evidence that a particular item appears in a particular paper. The official marking-scheme archive is likewise not an item match until the correct subject/year/paper code and accepted answer are compared.
5. Accordingly, no item was upgraded to authenticated PYQ status.

REQUIRED EVIDENCE TO AUTHENTICATE ANY ITEM
- Original CBSE paper PDF for the exact year and subject (Mathematics Basic or Standard, as applicable), including paper code/set.
- Corresponding official CBSE marking scheme/final answer key.
- Item-level matching of question wording, options/variants, question number, and accepted answer.
- Reviewer identity/date and disposition for any variant or ambiguity.

OFFICIAL SOURCE ROUTES
- CBSE previous-year question papers: https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/question-paper.html
- CBSE marking schemes: https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/marking-scheme.html
- CBSE sample question paper archive (sample papers, not proof of annual exam provenance): https://cbseacademic.nic.in/sqp_archive.html

SCOPE LIMITATION
The official archive pages were checked as source-discovery routes. The original question-paper ZIP/PDF files and corresponding official marking schemes were not downloaded and compared item by item in this run. Therefore this is a provenance-claim containment audit, not a completed item-level PYQ authentication exercise.
