## Key issues addressed

1. Human earlobe attachment was incorrectly presented as a universally simple dominant/recessive trait. The items now explain the complexity or use a clear pea-plant Mendelian model.
2. The gene definition was broadened to include functional RNA products, while retaining the protein-focused NCERT teaching context.
3. Sex-changing snails are no longer described as having no genetic influence; the wording distinguishes flexible reproductive sex from a fixed human-like XX/XY system.
4. Human sex-probability questions now explicitly identify the simplified equal-probability XX/XY model and avoid claiming actual birth ratios are exactly 50:50.
5. Questions on independent assortment now state the unlinked-loci assumption.
6. A question about human height and ABO blood groups now acknowledges that height is polygenic and uses an explicitly simplified Mendelian model.
7. The reptile temperature-dependent sex determination and sea-turtle warming questions were qualified to avoid generalising across all reptiles or predicting inevitable extinction.
8. The sex-determination wording was made neutral and avoids assigning blame to either parent.
