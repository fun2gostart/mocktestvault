### Scope decisions
The core-aligned provisional group is limited to direct applications using standard angles 30°, 45°, and 60°, with no more than two right triangles, plus closely related angle-of-elevation/depression concepts.
The enrichment group comprises:
- Complementary-angle formulas with arbitrary angles (including the related numerical examples).
- Cloud/reflection model with arbitrary α and β.
- Window/house model with arbitrary θ and φ.
- Ladder displacement relation using general α and β.
- Apparent angular diameter / tangent-to-circle geometry.
- Speed/time extensions involving moving cars or birds.
- General tangent non-linearity and complementary-angle proof items.

### Important limitations
This is an AI-assisted mathematical and curriculum screen, not a substitute for teacher/SME approval. A valid answer index and a consistent derivation do not establish source authenticity, pedagogical quality, diagram sufficiency, or suitability for every board-paper format. Items remain on hold until a qualified reviewer records a decision.
