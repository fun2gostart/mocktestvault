MockTestVault production release v2.8
Date: 2026-11-05
Baseline: mocktestvault.com_organic_seo_v2.7.zip

Implemented in this release
1. Fixed the contact form inquiry-field ID mismatch and added the optional Chapter / Question ID to the email draft.
2. Removed the contact form's automatic mail-client redirect; visitors now review and click the email draft link. The generated reference is not represented as a server-side ticket.
3. Removed persistent localStorage storage of contact and question-discrepancy report text and email addresses.
4. Reworked Google Analytics consent loading: consent defaults are established before tag configuration; the Google tag is not requested until explicit Accept All consent; Essential Only leaves analytics disabled; page_view is emitted after the tag is loaded.
5. Updated the privacy notice to reflect actual localStorage keys, email-draft behaviour, consent-controlled analytics and hosting-log uncertainty.
6. Replaced unsupported claims of comprehensive expert verification, authenticated official PYQs, and verified rationales with accurate practice/source-status disclosures.
7. Relabelled CBSE year-labelled sets and related cards as practice sets with unauthenticated provenance; removed official-solved-paper wording where it overstated the evidence.
8. Corrected stale question-bank count displays and removed the false "Verified" label from the formula reference badge.
9. Corrected remaining year-labelled Class 10, Class 12 and SSC 2023 page titles, badges, schema descriptions and result labels so these sets are not presented as authenticated official papers.
10. Cleaned residual official/verified wording on SSC 2023, CBSE Class 10 and CBSE Class 12 year-labelled practice pages and diagnostic result notices.
11. Removed the placeholder ads.txt publisher ID. No AdSense publisher declaration or live ad units are configured in this release.
12. Applied the consent banner to the standalone policy/format pages and added styling on the standalone progress page.
13. Published v2.8-named copies of the question verification and provenance registers; the underlying question-bank verification status remains unchanged.
14. Added explicit independent-practice and answer-verification caveats to the Class 10 Science and Social Science 80-question drills; removed unsupported exhaustive-coverage and official-marking-scheme language.
15. Extended consent coverage to the source-review and TOEFL 2026 format-reference pages and styled the consent notice on the standalone progress page.
16. Added the v2.8 production QA report with archive-level regression results and explicit external-validation limitations.
17. Removed residual exhaustive-coverage language from the remaining CBSE 2023 year-labelled practice pages; disclosures now state that coverage is selected-topic practice and not an exhaustive curriculum assessment.

Sitemap audit
- The sitemap contains 155 unique URLs.
- 157 HTML canonical URLs exist. The two excluded canonical URLs are 404.html and progress.html; both are marked noindex.
- Therefore, the sitemap already covers all indexable canonical HTML pages. No extra noindex URLs were added.

Explicitly not certified by v2.8
- The 6,404 question records have not all been independently re-solved by qualified subject-matter reviewers.
- The 954 year/shift-labelled items remain unauthenticated against original papers and official answer keys.
- No AdSense account approval, publisher ID, certified CMP implementation or live monetisation test is included.
