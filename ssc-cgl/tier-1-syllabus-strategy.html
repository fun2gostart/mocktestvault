1. General Intelligence & Reasoning (25 Qs • 50 Marks)

Practice Reasoning Drills →

Reasoning is the highest-scoring section of SSC CGL. A top-percentile candidate should target 23–25 correct answers in 14 to 16 minutes.

Topic / Sub-skill Expected Weightage Difficulty Level Core Concepts & Patterns Tested
Analogies & Classification 4–6 Questions Easy to Medium Letter cluster shifts (+3, +5), product of consecutive integers, semantic antonyms/synonyms.
Number & Letter Series 3–4 Questions Medium Differences of squares/cubes, alternating operations (×2 + 1, ×2 + 2), cyclic alphabetical steps.
Coding-Decoding 2–3 Questions Easy to Medium Direct letter-to-digit substitution, opposite letter values (AZ, BY), reverse positional sums.
Syllogisms & Logic 2 Questions Medium to Hard Categorical syllogisms with 3 statements, 'Some... not' deductions, possibility vs certainty.
Blood Relations & Direction 2–3 Questions Medium Coded blood relations (P + Q × R), Pythagorean displacement (North-East, 10 km).
Non-Verbal & Visual Reasoning 4–5 Questions Easy Paper folding & punching, lateral mirror inversion, embedded figures, counting triangles.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (SSC CGL Tier 1)

Is there a sectional cutoff in SSC CGL Tier 1?
No. There is no sectional cutoff in SSC CGL Tier 1. Qualification for Tier 2 is determined purely by your aggregate normalized score across all four sections (out of 200 marks).
Does Tier 1 score count towards the final merit list?
Under the revised SSC CGL scheme, Tier 1 is qualifying in nature. While your Tier 1 score determines whether you qualify to write Tier 2, the final merit ranking and department allocation are based solely on your Tier 2 performance.
