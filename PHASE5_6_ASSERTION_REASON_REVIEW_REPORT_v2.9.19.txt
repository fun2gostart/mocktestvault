KEY CORRECTIONS
1. Federalism / Article 254: added the President's-assent exception for a State law on a Concurrent List subject.
2. Constitutional amendment procedure: stated the special-majority threshold and ratification by at least half of the States more precisely.
3. Local government reservations: clarified that the Constitution mandates reservations for SCs, STs and women; State Legislatures may provide reservation for backward classes.
4. Women's representation: separated the 73rd Amendment (Panchayats) from the 74th Amendment (Municipalities).
5. Election law: aligned the religious-appeal question with Section 123(3) of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, rather than claiming a blanket ban on all religious places or objects.
6. Anti-defection: removed the claim that any defection causes immediate loss of a seat; included the Tenth Schedule grounds, process and merger exception.
7. Collateral: changed the universal claim that all bank loans require collateral to the NCERT-accurate position that lenders may require it.
8. CRR: removed the old 15% textbook cash-holding illustration as a current CRR figure. The RBI website listed CRR at 3% on 06 November 2026. CRR changes over time.
9. Globalisation/WTO: removed the inaccurate statement that WTO rules compel developing countries alone to dismantle trade barriers.
10. Lifelines/Geography: corrected the Kolkata port dredging explanation, clarified the slurry-pipeline example, and removed absolute or overbroad claims about zero dust pollution and air travel.
11. Democracy: removed the inaccurate term 'electoral recall' from the explanation of democratic correction mechanisms.
12. Sectors/Economics: clarified the distinction between organised enterprises and informalisation of employment.
13. Money and credit: aligned currency, collateral, demand deposits and informal-credit wording more closely with NCERT and the applicable regulatory framework.
14. European Union: clarified that Brussels is a principal administrative centre hosting the Commission and Council; the EU does not have one formally designated capital.
15. SEZs: clarified that the five-year tax exemption is the textbook's described policy provision, not a universal current tax benefit.
