📘 NTA JEE Main Examination Architecture & Speed Strategy

The Joint Entrance Examination (Main) conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) evaluates analytical speed, precision, and foundational mastery across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. This sectional speed drill is designed to train rapid problem elimination under strict +4 / -1 negative marking constraints.

⚛️ Physics Mechanics & Electrodynamics Dimensional checks, energy conservation shortcuts, pure rolling constraints, resonant LCR circuits, and wave interference conditions.
🧪 Chemistry Inorganic & Reaction Kinetics Crystal field stabilization energies, VSEPR geometries, thermodynamic spontaneity criteria, and nucleophilic substitution mechanisms.
📐 Mathematics Calculus & 3D Vectors L'Hôpital / Taylor expansions for limits, definite integral symmetry properties, shortest distance between skew lines, and Bayes' Theorem.
⏱️ Time Management Benchmark Allocating an average of 1.8 minutes per question allows thorough reading, formula substitution, and double-checking calculation signs.
⚖️ Published Exam Scoring Pattern & Practice Strategy: • Marks per Correct Answer: +4 marks.
• Negative Marking Penalty: -1 mark deducted for every incorrect choice. Unattempted questions receive 0 marks.
• Target Raw Score: 70+/100 marks represents a strong practice benchmark for JEE Advanced qualification readiness.
• Elimination Rule: Never make blind guesses; eliminate at least two options before risking negative penalty points.
