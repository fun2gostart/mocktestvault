📘 NTA NEET UG Medical Entrance Architecture & High-Yield Strategy

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate) conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) determines admissions to MBBS, BDS, and allied medical courses across India. This sectional drill replicates the rigorous pacing and diagnostic precision needed to secure seats in prestigious government medical colleges (GMCs) and AIIMS institutions.

🧬 Biology (Botany & Zoology) 100% NCERT verbatim mastery across cell biology, genetics, transcription/translation, human endocrine feedback loops, and ecosystem energy pyramids.
🧪 Chemistry (Physical, Inorganic & Organic) Henry's law, common ion solubility product, Markovnikov peroxide effect, Hinsberg amine tests, and coordination complex IUPAC nomenclature.
⚛️ Physics (Core Mechanics & Modern Physics) Dimensional analysis, banked roads, Torricelli's Law, Wien's displacement, thin lens combinations, radioactive decay, and semiconductor forward bias.
⏱️ Pacing & Negative Marking Target solving 25 Biology questions in 15-20 minutes to preserve generous calculation time for multi-step Physics numerical problems.
⚖️ Official Marking Scheme & Strategy: • Marks per Correct Answer: +4 marks (Max Marks: 180).
• Negative Marking Penalty: -1 mark deducted for every incorrect choice. Unattempted questions receive 0 marks.
• Target Score: 130+/180 marks demonstrates top-tier competence for all-India quota (AIQ) medical seats.
• Accuracy Rule: Consistent high accuracy (85%+) outweighs speculative guessing due to the punishing -1 penalty.
