📘 CBSE Class 10 Social Science (Geography) — Chapter 11: Manufacturing Industries Blueprint

Importance of manufacturing as the backbone of economic development, industrial location factors, classification of industries (agro-based, mineral-based), iron and steel industry, and industrial environmental pollution control.

🏭 Economic Significance & Agglomeration Manufacturing transforms raw materials into valuable finished goods. Agglomeration economies occur when industries cluster in urban centers to share banking, labor, and transport infrastructure.
🧵 Agro-Based Industries Cotton textiles (first successful mill Mumbai 1854, concentrated in Gujarat/Maharashtra due to raw cotton, port, humid climate). Jute textiles (first mill Rishra near Kolkata 1855, Hugli basin).
🔩 Iron & Steel Mineral Industry Basic heavy industry requiring raw materials in ratio 4 Iron ore : 2 Coking coal : 1 Limestone. Concentrated in Chhota Nagpur plateau due to low-cost iron ore, coking coal, and cheap labor.
🌊 Industrial Pollution & Environmental Control Air pollution (SO2, CO from chimneys), Water pollution (organic/inorganic effluents), Thermal pollution (hot water dumped into rivers). Control: Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) and electrostatic precipitators.

📝 Sample Board Examination Problems & Official Solutions

Q1 (Definition of Manufacturing): Production of goods in large quantities after processing from raw materials to more valuable products is called:

  • (A) Agriculture
  • (B) Manufacturing
  • (C) Tertiary distribution
  • (D) Mining
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (B) — Manufacturing

Explanation: Production of goods in large quantities after processing from raw materials to more valuable products is called manufacturing. It falls under the secondary sector of the economy.

NCERT Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography, Chapter 6, Section 1

Q2 (Economic Strength & Manufacturing): The economic strength of a country is measured by the development of which of the following sectors?

  • (A) Manufacturing industries
  • (B) Subsistence farming
  • (C) Import of raw materials
  • (D) Gold reserves only
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (A) — Manufacturing industries

Explanation: Manufacturing industries not only help in modernising agriculture but also reduce heavy dependence on agricultural income by providing jobs in secondary and tertiary sectors. Hence, the economic strength of a country is measured by its manufacturing development.

NCERT Source: NCERT Class 10 Geography, Chapter 6, Section 1.1
