Detailed Solution Review

📘 CBSE Class 10 Social Science (Political Science) — Chapter 16: Political Parties Blueprint

Need and functions of political parties, party systems (one-party, two-party, multi-party), criteria for recognition as National and State political parties by Election Commission, and major challenges facing parties.

🏛️ Three Components & Functions Components: The leaders, active members, and followers. Functions: Contest elections, put forward policies/programs, form and run government, play role of opposition, shape public opinion.
🗳️ National vs State Party Criteria National Party: 6% of valid votes in Lok Sabha or Assembly elections in 4 states + at least 4 Lok Sabha seats. State Party: 6% votes in state legislative assembly + at least 2 assembly seats.
⚠️ Challenges to Political Parties 1. Lack of internal democracy (concentration of power in top leaders); 2. Dynastic succession; 3. Growing role of money and muscle power; 4. Lack of meaningful choices for voters.
🛠️ Electoral Reforms & Anti-Defection Anti-Defection Law (52nd Amendment) disqualifies legislators changing party after election. Mandatory affidavit declaring criminal cases and financial assets. IT returns filing.

📝 Illustrative Practice Questions & Worked Explanations

Q1 (Political Party Definition): A political party is defined as a group of people who come together to:

  • (A) Organize religious festivals and pilgrimages
  • (B) Operate private commercial transport networks
  • (C) Provide judicial arbitration in civil disputes
  • (D) Contest elections and hold power in the government
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (D) — Contest elections and hold power in the government

Explanation: A political party is a group of people who come together to contest elections and hold power in the government. They agree on some policies and programmes for the society with a view to promote the collective good.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Civics, Chapter 4, Section 1

Q2 (Three Components of a Political Party): A political party has three core components. Which of the following correctly identifies all three?

  • (A) The prime minister, the supreme court judges, and the police
  • (B) The leaders, the active members, and the followers
  • (C) The president, the governor, and the chief election commissioner
  • (D) The civil servants, the army generals, and the media reporters
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (B) — The leaders, the active members, and the followers

Explanation: A political party has three components: (i) the leaders, (ii) the active members, and (iii) the followers.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Civics, Chapter 4, Section 1
