3. A practical revision workflow

  1. Check the current syllabus. Confirm the subject, course level (where applicable), chapters, prescribed content and assessment notes for 2026–27.
  2. Attempt the official sample paper. Use the paper for the current subject and session, under the time and conditions stated by CBSE.
  3. Review the official marking scheme. Compare the expected steps, method marks and answer format; do not rely on a third-party mock score as a board prediction.
  4. Practise weak topics. Use chapter-wise questions for additional repetition, then return to the official paper to check whether the underlying concept is secure.
  5. Recheck updates. CBSE can publish circulars or revised materials during the session. The official portal takes precedence over third-party summaries.

Science (086): important exclusions and formative-only content

  • The 2026–27 curriculum states that Heredity and Evolution and Periodic Classification of Elements are not assessed in the year-end examination.
  • The curriculum identifies Motor, Electromagnetic Induction and Electric Generator as formative-only topics. Do not interpret this as excluding every topic in the broader Magnetic Effects of Current chapter.
  • The official unit-wise theory allocation is Chemical Substances 25, World of Living 25, Natural Phenomena 12, Effects of Current 13 and Natural Resources 5 marks (80 total).

Open official CBSE Science curriculum 2026–27 ↗.

Social Science (087): board, periodic, project and map-only scope

  • The Age of Industrialisation: periodic assessment only.
  • The Making of a Global World: History subtopics 1 to 1.3 are board-exam content; the later listed subtopics are part of the interdisciplinary project.
  • Lifelines of National Economy: only map pointing is evaluated in the board examination.
  • Globalisation and the Indian Economy: the board-exam scope is “What is Globalisation?” and “Factors that have enabled Globalisation”; the remaining listed areas are assigned to the interdisciplinary project.
  • Consumer Rights: project work.
