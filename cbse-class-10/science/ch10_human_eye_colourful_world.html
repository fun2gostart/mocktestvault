Detailed Solution Review

📘 CBSE Class 10 Science (Physics) — Chapter 10: The Human Eye and the Colourful World Blueprint

Anatomy of the human eye, power of accommodation, common refractive vision defects (myopia, hypermetropia, presbyopia) with optical lens corrections, prism refraction, dispersion, and atmospheric scattering phenomena.

👁️ Eye Anatomy & Accommodation Ciliary muscles adjust crystalline lens curvature to focus near and distant objects (accommodation). Normal near point = 25 cm (least distance of distinct vision); far point = infinity.
👓 Vision Defects & Lens Corrections Myopia (nearsightedness): image forms in front of retina, corrected by concave lens. Hypermetropia (farsightedness): image forms behind retina, corrected by convex lens. Presbyopia: bifocal lenses.
🌈 Prism Dispersion & Rainbow Triangular glass prism splits white light into spectrum (VIBGYOR) because different wavelengths travel at different speeds in glass (red deviates least, violet deviates most). Rainbow involves refraction, dispersion, and internal reflection.
✨ Atmospheric Refraction & Tyndall Effect Twinkling of stars and advanced sunrise/delayed sunset (by ~2 mins) result from continuous atmospheric refraction. Blue sky color and reddish sunset result from Rayleigh scattering (I ∝ 1/λ⁴).

📝 Illustrative Practice Questions & Worked Explanations

Q1 (Defects of Vision: Myopia (Near-sightedness)): A student sitting on the back bench of a classroom can read their notebook easily but cannot clearly read the letters written on the blackboard. What defect of vision does this student have, and what type of lens corrects it?

  • (A) Hypermetropia, corrected by a convex lens
  • (B) Presbyopia, corrected by a cylindrical lens
  • (C) Myopia (short-sightedness), corrected by a concave lens
  • (D) Astigmatism, corrected by a bifocal lens
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (C) — Myopia (short-sightedness), corrected by a concave lens

Explanation: A person with myopia can see nearby objects clearly but cannot see distant objects distinctly. The far point of a myopic eye is closer than infinity, and parallel light rays focus in front of the retina. A concave lens of suitable focal length diverges incoming rays so they focus sharply on the retina.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 10, Section 10.2 Defects of Vision and Their Correction

Q2 (Refraction through a Prism: Dispersion of Light): When a narrow beam of white light passes through a triangular glass prism, it splits into a band of seven colors. Which color of light suffers the MAXIMUM deviation, and which suffers the LEAST deviation?

  • (A) Violet deviates most, Red deviates least
  • (B) Red deviates most, Violet deviates least
  • (C) Green deviates most, Yellow deviates least
  • (D) All seven colors deviate by the exact same angle
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (A) — Violet deviates most, Red deviates least

Explanation: In glass, different colors of light travel at different speeds. Violet light has the shortest wavelength and travels slowest, suffering the maximum refraction/deviation. Red light has the longest visible wavelength and travels fastest, bending the least. Hence, Violet is at the bottom and Red is at the top of the spectrum.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 10, Section 10.3 Refraction of Light Through a Prism
