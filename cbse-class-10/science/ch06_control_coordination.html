Detailed Solution Review

📘 CBSE Class 10 Science (Biology) — Chapter 6: Control and Coordination Blueprint

Covers neural coordination in animals, neuron anatomy, electrical impulse transmission across synapses, reflex arcs, human brain divisions, and chemical coordination via plant phytohormones and animal endocrine glands.

🧠 Neuron & Reflex Arc Dendrite receives stimulus -> Cyton -> Axon -> Synapse (neurotransmitter release). Reflex arc pathway: Receptor -> Sensory neuron -> Relay neuron (spinal cord) -> Motor neuron -> Effector muscle.
🏛️ Human Brain Anatomy Forebrain (Cerebrum: thought, memory, voluntary actions), Midbrain (visual/auditory reflexes), Hindbrain (Cerebellum: posture/balance; Medulla: involuntary vomiting/BP; Pons: respiration rate).
🌱 Plant Tropisms & Hormones Phototropism, Geotropism, Hydrotropism, Thigmotropism. Auxin promotes cell elongation towards light; Gibberellins promote stem growth; Cytokinins stimulate cell division; Abscisic acid (ABA) causes wilting.
💉 Endocrine System & Feedback Pituitary (Growth hormone), Thyroid (Thyroxine requires iodine), Pancreas (Insulin lowers blood glucose), Adrenals (Adrenaline fight-or-flight). Regulated by negative feedback loops.

📝 Illustrative Practice Questions & Worked Explanations

Q1 (Nerve Impulse Conduction Pathway): In a human neuron, an electrical nerve impulse travels through which sequence of anatomical structures?

  • (A) Dendrite → Cell body (cyton) → Axon → Axonal nerve ending
  • (B) Axon → Cell body → Dendrite → Synapse
  • (C) Cell body → Dendrite → Axon → Nucleus
  • (D) Nerve ending → Axon → Cell body → Dendrite
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (A) — Dendrite → Cell body (cyton) → Axon → Axonal nerve ending

Explanation: Information is acquired at the dendritic tip, sets off an electrical impulse that travels through the cell body, then along the axon to its terminal end. At the synapse, electrical signals trigger chemical neurotransmitter release.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 6, Section 6.1.1

Q2 (Brain Anatomy & Cerebellum): Which specific region of the human brain is responsible for maintaining posture, equilibrium, and precision of voluntary muscle actions like riding a bicycle?

  • (A) Cerebrum in the forebrain
  • (B) Medulla in the hindbrain
  • (C) Cerebellum in the hindbrain
  • (D) Hypothalamus
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (C) — Cerebellum in the hindbrain

Explanation: The cerebellum (part of the hindbrain) coordinates voluntary movements, posture, balance, and fine motor precision (e.g. walking a straight line, picking up a pen).

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Science, Chapter 6, Section 6.1.3
