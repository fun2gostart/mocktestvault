Detailed Solution Review

📘 CBSE Class 10 Mathematics (Geometry) — Chapter 10: Circles Blueprint

Tangents to a circle, point of contact, Theorem 10.1 (radius is perpendicular to tangent at point of contact), and Theorem 10.2 (lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal).

📍 Tangent & Point of Contact A tangent to a circle is a straight line that touches the circle at exactly one point (point of contact). Secant cuts in two points; chord joins two points.
📐 Theorem 10.1 (Perpendicularity) The tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact: OP ⊥ AB where P is point of contact and O is center.
📏 Theorem 10.2 (Lengths of Tangents) The lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal: If tangents PA and PB are drawn from external point P, then PA = PB. Also ∠OPA = ∠OPB.
⭕ Quadrilateral Circumscribing Circle If a quadrilateral ABCD circumscribes a circle touching at P, Q, R, S, then AB + CD = AD + BC (sum of opposite sides are equal).

📝 Sample Board Examination Problems & Official Solutions

Q1 (Theorem 10.1 Radius Tangent Perpendicularity): The tangent at any point of a circle is always perpendicular to which geometric line through the point of contact?

  • (A) The radius through the point of contact
  • (B) The chord passing through the point
  • (C) The line segment connecting to the opposite circumference
  • (D) Any secant line drawn to the circle
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (A) — The radius through the point of contact

Explanation: According to Theorem 10.1 in NCERT Mathematics, the tangent at any point of a circle is strictly perpendicular to the radius drawn through the point of contact. Proof: Let line l be the tangent touching the circle at point P with center O. Take any other point Q on line l distinct from P. Because l is a tangent, Q must lie outside the circle. Therefore, distance OQ is strictly greater than the radius OP (OQ > OP). Since this holds for every point on l other than P, OP is the shortest distance from center O to line l, proving OP is perpendicular to l.

NCERT Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 10, Theorem 10.1

Q2 (Theorem 10.2 External Tangent Length Equality): From an external point P, two tangents PA and PB are drawn to a circle. What is the relation between the lengths of PA and PB?

  • (A) PA > PB
  • (B) PA = PB
  • (C) PA + PB = Radius
  • (D) PA = 2 PB
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (B) — PA = PB

Explanation: Theorem 10.2 of CBSE Class 10 states: The lengths of the two tangents drawn from a single external point to a circle are equal (PA = PB). Proof: Let PA and PB be tangents drawn from point P to a circle with center O, touching at points A and B. Join OA, OB, and OP. In right-angled triangles OPA and OPB: angle OAP = angle OBP = 90 degrees (Theorem 10.1); hypotenuse OP is common (OP = OP); and radius OA = OB. By the RHS congruence criterion, triangle OPA is congruent to triangle OPB, which immediately implies that PA = PB by CPCT.

NCERT Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 10, Theorem 10.2
