Detailed Solution Review

📘 CBSE Class 10 Mathematics (Algebra) — Chapter 2: Polynomials Blueprint

Covers linear, quadratic, and cubic polynomials, geometrical significance of zeroes from Cartesian graphs, and the algebraic relationship between the coefficients and roots of a quadratic polynomial.

📈 Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes The zeroes of polynomial p(x) are the exact x-coordinates of the points where the graph y = p(x) intersects the horizontal x-axis. A degree-n polynomial has at most n real zeroes.
📐 Quadratic Roots & Coefficients For quadratic polynomial ax² + bx + c = 0 with zeroes α and β: Sum of zeroes: α + β = -b / a. Product of zeroes: αβ = c / a.
✏️ Constructing Quadratic Polynomials Given sum S = (α + β) and product P = αβ, the corresponding family of quadratic polynomials is given by: k [ x² - (α + β)x + αβ ] where k is any non-zero real constant.
🔄 Higher Symmetric Root Identities Frequently tested algebraic expansions: α² + β² = (α + β)² - 2αβ; 1/α + 1/β = (α + β)/(αβ); and α³ + β³ = (α + β)³ - 3αβ(α + β).

Q1 (Relationship between Zeroes and Coefficients): If alpha and beta are the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial p(x) = x^2 - 2x - 8, what are the sum (alpha + beta) and product (alpha * beta) of its zeroes?

  • (A) Sum = 2, Product = -8
  • (B) Sum = -2, Product = 8
  • (C) Sum = -2, Product = -8
  • (D) Sum = 8, Product = 2
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (A) — Sum = 2, Product = -8

Explanation: For any standard quadratic polynomial p(x) = ax^2 + bx + c with zeroes alpha and beta, the fundamental relationships between zeroes and coefficients state: Sum of zeroes (alpha + beta) = -b/a, and Product of zeroes (alpha * beta) = c/a. In p(x) = x^2 - 2x - 8, coefficients are a = 1, b = -2, and c = -8. Applying the formulas: Sum = -(-2)/1 = 2, and Product = -8/1 = -8. To cross-verify by factorisation: x^2 - 4x + 2x - 8 = (x - 4)(x + 2) = 0 gives zeroes 4 and -2. Sum: 4 + (-2) = 2; Product: 4 * (-2) = -8.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 2, Section 2.3

Q2 (Formation of Quadratic Polynomial): What is the quadratic polynomial whose sum of zeroes is -3 and product of zeroes is 2?

  • (A) x^2 - 3x + 2
  • (B) x^2 + 3x - 2
  • (C) x^2 - 3x - 2
  • (D) x^2 + 3x + 2
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (D) — x^2 + 3x + 2

Explanation: Per NCERT Class 10 Chapter 2, a quadratic polynomial with a given sum (S) and product (P) of zeroes is expressed as k[x^2 - Sx + P], where k is any non-zero real constant. Here, we are given Sum (S) = -3 and Product (P) = 2. Substituting these values into the polynomial form: p(x) = k[x^2 - (-3)x + 2] = k(x^2 + 3x + 2). Taking the simplest non-zero real constant k = 1 yields the standard quadratic polynomial x^2 + 3x + 2. Factoring (x + 1)(x + 2) = 0 confirms zeroes are -1 and -2, whose sum is -3 and product is 2.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 2, Section 2.3
