📘 CBSE Class 10 Mathematics (Coordinate Geometry) — Chapter 7: Coordinate Geometry Blueprint

Cartesian coordinate geometry on 2D planes: distance formula, distance from origin, collinearity tests, section formula for internal division of line segments, and midpoint coordinates.

📏 Distance Formula Distance between P(x₁, y₁) and Q(x₂, y₂): d = √[ (x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)² ]. Distance of point P(x, y) from origin O(0, 0) is simply √(x² + y²).
⚖️ Collinearity Test Three points A, B, C are collinear if the sum of lengths of any two segments equals the third segment (e.g. AB + BC = AC).
✂️ Section Formula (Internal Division) Coordinates of point P dividing line segment AB in ratio m₁:m₂: P = [ (m₁x₂ + m₂x₁)/(m₁ + m₂), (m₁y₂ + m₂y₁)/(m₁ + m₂) ]. Shortcut ratio: use k:1.
📍 Midpoint & Centroid Coordinates Midpoint M of AB: M = [ (x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2 ]. Centroid of triangle ABC: G = [ (x₁ + x₂ + x₃)/3, (y₁ + y₂ + y₃)/3 ].

📝 Sample Board Examination Problems & Official Solutions

Q1 (Distance Formula Standard Calculation): What is the distance between the points P(2, 3) and Q(4, 1)?

  • (A) 2 sqrt(2) units
  • (B) 4 units
  • (C) 8 units
  • (D) 2 units
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (A) — 2 sqrt(2) units

Explanation: By the Cartesian distance formula derived directly from the Pythagorean theorem, the straight-line distance d between two points P(x1, y1) and Q(x2, y2) is given by d = sqrt((x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2). Here, the coordinates are P(2, 3) and Q(4, 1), so x1 = 2, y1 = 3, x2 = 4, y2 = 1. Substituting into the distance formula: d = sqrt((4 - 2)^2 + (1 - 3)^2) = sqrt(2^2 + (-2)^2) = sqrt(4 + 4) = sqrt(8). Simplifying the radical gives sqrt(4 * 2) = 2*sqrt(2) units, matching the hypotenuse of the corresponding right-angled step triangle.

NCERT Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 7, Section 7.2

Q2 (Distance from Origin): The distance of the point P(-6, 8) from the origin (0, 0) is:

  • (A) 8 units
  • (B) 6 units
  • (C) 14 units
  • (D) 10 units
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (D) — 10 units

Explanation: The distance of any arbitrary point P(x, y) on the Cartesian plane from the origin O(0, 0) is derived from the distance formula: OP = sqrt((x - 0)^2 + (y - 0)^2) = sqrt(x^2 + y^2). For the given point P(-6, 8), we have x = -6 and y = 8. Substituting these coordinate values into the origin distance formula gives: OP = sqrt((-6)^2 + 8^2) = sqrt(36 + 64) = sqrt(100) = 10 units. Since distance represents a physical scalar displacement, the principal square root is strictly positive, giving exactly 10 units.

NCERT Source: NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 7, Section 7.2
