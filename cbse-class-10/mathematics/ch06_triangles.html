Detailed Solution Review

📘 CBSE Class 10 Mathematics (Geometry) — Chapter 6: Triangles Blueprint

Similarity of geometric figures, Thales Theorem (Basic Proportionality Theorem - BPT) and its converse, criteria for similarity of triangles (AAA, SSS, SAS), and multi-step geometric proofs.

📐 Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting other two sides, it divides them in the same ratio: AD/DB = AE/EC in ΔABC with DE || BC.
🔄 Converse of BPT If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line must be strictly parallel to the third side.
👥 Criteria for Triangle Similarity AAA (Angle-Angle-Angle or AA criterion), SSS (Side-Side-Side proportional sides), SAS (Two sides proportional and included angle equal: AB/DE = AC/DF and ∠A = ∠D).
📏 Properties of Similar Triangles If ΔABC ~ ΔDEF, then corresponding angles are equal (∠A=∠D, ∠B=∠E, ∠C=∠F) and corresponding sides, perimeters, medians, and altitudes are in identical ratios.

📝 Illustrative Practice Questions & Worked Explanations

Q1 (Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT)): In triangle ABC, DE is parallel to BC with D on AB and E on AC. If AD = 1.5 cm, DB = 3 cm, and AE = 1 cm, what is the length of EC?

  • (A) 2.5 cm
  • (B) 2 cm
  • (C) 3 cm
  • (D) 1.5 cm
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (B) — 2 cm

Explanation: By the Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT, also known as Thales Theorem), if a straight line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the exact same ratio: AD / DB = AE / EC. Given values: AD = 1.5 cm, DB = 3 cm, and AE = 1 cm. Substituting into the BPT ratio: 1.5 / 3 = 1 / EC => 1/2 = 1 / EC. Cross-multiplying yields EC = 2 * 1 = 2 cm, which represents the remaining lower segment of side AC.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 6, Section 6.2, Theorem 6.1

Q2 (Similarity Criteria Definition): Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal and their corresponding sides are:

  • (A) Equal in length
  • (B) Mutually perpendicular
  • (C) In the same ratio (proportional)
  • (D) Parallel to each other
🔍 View Correct Answer & Step-by-Step Marking Explanation

Correct Answer: Option (C) — In the same ratio (proportional)

Explanation: In Euclidean geometry, two polygons (or triangles) having the same number of sides are defined to be 'similar' if and only if two simultaneous geometric conditions are satisfied: (1) All their corresponding interior angles are strictly equal in measure, and (2) All their corresponding sides are in the exact same proportion (their side lengths share a common constant scaling ratio). When two triangles are similar, they possess identical shape but may differ in physical spatial scale or size.

Topic reference (not item-level provenance): NCERT Class 10 Mathematics, Chapter 6, Section 6.1
