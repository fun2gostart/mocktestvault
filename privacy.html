Privacy Policy

📅 Effective Date: October 30, 2026
🔄 Last Updated: November 3, 2026
🛡️ Zero Server Database Logging

1. Introduction & Core Privacy Promise

Welcome to MockTestVault (accessible at https://mocktestvault.com/). We respect the privacy of every student, educator, and test aspirant who utilizes our computer-based examination simulators. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards regarding data handling, client-side execution, browser local storage, advertising cookies, and your legal rights.

Unlike conventional educational portals, MockTestVault is engineered as an open-access, client-side utility. We do not operate user accounts, do not mandate phone number registration, and never harvest personal student profiles.

2. In-Browser Execution Architecture (Zero Server Storage of Test Data)

The Client-Side Privacy Guarantee: When you start, answer, pause, or complete an examination on MockTestVault:
  • Your selected answers, skipped questions, and marked-for-review flags are processed 100% locally in your device's browser memory (RAM) via pure JavaScript state machines.
  • No test score, question response, or examination timestamp is ever transmitted to or stored on our web servers or external cloud databases.
  • When you close your browser tab or click "Reset Test", all active session data is immediately discarded.
  • Scope Note: This client-side execution model applies specifically to examination session interaction and scoring data. Standard anonymous web server HTTP request logs (IP address, user agent) and aggregated usage metrics operate as disclosed in Section 5.

3. Browser Local Storage & Preference Cookies

MockTestVault utilizes modern HTML5 localStorage exclusively to preserve non-identifying client preferences and offline self-study progress across visits. Specifically, we store:

  • mtv_theme: Remembers your display theme selection ('light' or 'dark' mode) to prevent flashing un-styled content upon page load.
  • mtv_sound: Records your Web Audio synthesizer preference ('true' or 'false' for millisecond audio feedback).
  • mtv_font_scale: Stores your customized typography zoom scale (between 85% and 125%) for enhanced readability.
  • mtv_bookmarks: Locally saves question IDs you explicitly flagged via the "⭐ Bookmark Question" button so you can filter and revise them later.
  • mtv_attempt_history: Records your local examination score summaries (date, score, percentage, elapsed time) to render your self-study progression chart without cloud account sync.
  • mtv_question_reports: If you use the "Report Question / Suggest Correction" feature, your report text and optional email address are drafted and preserved exclusively on your device's local storage before opening your system email application. No automated background transmission takes place.
  • mcv_contact_reports: When submitting an academic discrepancy or support inquiry through our Contact Desk, a local archive of your submission summary is stored on your device's browser memory so you have a record of submitted reports. No automatic server database persistence occurs.
  • mtv_consent_choice: Records your consent preference ('accepted' or 'essential') via our client-side consent manager, determining whether anonymous telemetry and marketing storage are enabled in Google Consent Mode v2.

These keys reside exclusively within your local device browser sandbox and are never transmitted, synchronized, or uploaded to any remote server or third party. You can wipe these keys at any moment via your browser's "Clear Site Data" or "Clear Cookies & Cache" settings.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

To maintain MockTestVault as a 100% free educational platform accessible to students worldwide without mandatory subscriptions or paywalls, our operational model is designed to be supported by non-intrusive advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including Google Inc. (Google AdSense) upon deployment. In compliance with Google Publisher Policies, GDPR, and statutory advertising regulations, please note the following operational disclosures:

  • Third-Party Advertising Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to MockTestVault or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or contextual advertisements to users based on their visit to this site and other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's official privacy and advertising tools at:
    https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and customizing their preferences at Google Ads Settings.
  • In-Browser Consent Management & Google Consent Mode v2: MockTestVault deploys an active, interactive consent banner and Google Consent Mode v2 interface on every page. Visitors can choose between "Essential Only" (which sets analytics_storage, ad_storage, and personalization to denied) and "Accept All". You can review or modify your consent settings at any time by clicking Cookie Preferences.
  • Future Commercial Ad Serving & IAB TCF CMP: When commercial Google AdSense advertising is deployed, MockTestVault will integrate a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) supporting the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2) to govern European (EEA, UK, Switzerland) consent strings. Prior to commercial ad serving, only essential operational state is maintained.
  • Industry-Wide Consumer Choice Portals: You may also opt out of third-party interest-based advertising cookies across participating ad networks by visiting:
    • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): https://optout.aboutads.info/
    • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): https://optout.networkadvertising.org/
    • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/
  • Ad-Free Active Testing Sessions: MockTestVault enforces a strict policy that no disruptive, interstitial, or floating advertisements are displayed within active examination viewports. Timer countdowns, question palettes, and answer choices remain 100% distraction-free.
  • Zero-CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) Design: All ad units on MockTestVault are strictly sandboxed within fixed-height reserved wrappers to prevent page jumping, ensuring ad loading never distorts exam timers or misaligns question radio buttons.

5. Web Analytics & Server Access Logs

Like all standard web infrastructure, MockTestVault's edge servers may log basic non-personally identifiable diagnostic headers when an asset is requested:

  • Standard HTTP request metadata: Browser user-agent string, operating system version, referring URL, timestamp of request, and IP address (anonymized for aggregated geolocation metrics).
  • We may utilize anonymous aggregated telemetry tools (such as Google Analytics 4) to monitor platform load times, regional popularity of exam suites, and browser rendering compatibility. These tools do not record private exam answers, individual scores, or personally identifiable information.

6. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) & Minors

Given our curriculum coverage of secondary school syllabus (including CBSE Class 10), we adhere strictly to the principles of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and global minors' digital safety frameworks:

  • MockTestVault does not require, solicit, or collect personally identifiable information (PII) from any student, including children under 13 or 16 years of age.
  • Because no login credentials, names, email addresses, or phone numbers are requested to take tests, no children's data is maintained, compiled, or distributed.
  • Parents and educators can permit students to utilize the platform with total confidence in complete local anonymity.

7. Your Rights under GDPR, UK DPA & CCPA/CPRA

Under global data privacy regulations (including the European General Data Protection Regulation, the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act):

  • Right to Know & Access: Since we do not record student accounts, personal profiles, or test answers, we possess no identifiable database records to retrieve or disclose.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Users have total autonomy over their local device storage. Clearing your browser cache or cookies instantly deletes all stored preferences and test history.
  • "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" (CCPA/CPRA): MockTestVault does not sell, rent, monetize, or share personal user data with third-party data brokers or commercial marketers.
  • EU User Consent Policy: In regions governed by GDPR / ePrivacy directives, consent is requested before non-essential cookies (such as personalized advertising cookies) are deployed.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: MockTestVault treats all users identically, regardless of whether you accept or reject non-essential cookies.
