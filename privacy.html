Privacy & Data Practices

Privacy Policy

📅 Effective Date: October 30, 2026
🔄 Last Updated: November 5, 2026
🛡️ No student account system

1. Introduction & Privacy Overview

Welcome to MockTestVault (accessible at https://mocktestvault.com/). We respect the privacy of every student, educator, and test aspirant who utilizes our computer-based examination simulators. This Privacy Policy outlines our standards regarding data handling, client-side execution, browser local storage, advertising cookies, and your legal rights.

Unlike conventional educational portals, MockTestVault is engineered as an open-access, client-side utility. We do not operate user accounts, do not mandate phone number registration, and do not create student account profiles for practice use.

2. In-Browser Practice Processing

Practice Session Processing: When you start, answer, pause, or complete an examination on MockTestVault:
  • Your selected answers, skipped questions, and marked-for-review flags are processed 100% locally in your device's browser memory (RAM) via pure JavaScript state machines.
  • No test score, question response, or examination timestamp is ever transmitted to or stored on our web servers or external cloud databases.
  • When you close your browser tab or click "Reset Test", all active session data is immediately discarded.
  • Scope Note: This client-side execution model applies specifically to examination session interaction and scoring data. Standard anonymous web server HTTP request logs (IP address, user agent) and aggregated usage metrics operate as disclosed in Section 5.

3. Browser Local Storage & Device Preferences

MockTestVault uses browser localStorage for selected preferences and self-study features. These values remain in the current browser profile unless you clear site data. The keys used by the current site code include:

  • mtv_theme: your light/dark display preference.
  • mtv_sound: whether optional interface sound effects are enabled.
  • mtv_font_scale: your selected text-size scale.
  • mtv_bookmarks: question identifiers you bookmark for later revision.
  • mtv_attempt_history: local practice summaries, including test path, date/time, score, percentage and elapsed time. These records support the progress view and are not synced to a MockTestVault account.
  • mtv_consent_choice: your selected consent preference (accepted or essential).

In this release, contact-form and question-discrepancy text and email addresses are not saved by MockTestVault to localStorage. When you choose to open an email draft, the information is passed to your configured email application through a mailto: link. Your email provider and the recipient then handle the message under their own practices. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report.

To remove local preferences, bookmarks and attempt summaries, use your browser's site-data controls for mocktestvault.com. Removing site data also removes your saved preferences and practice history.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Advertising Readiness

The current website source release does not include Google AdSense ad-unit code. No AdSense publisher ID is configured in this release. Any future advertising deployment must be accompanied by an updated privacy notice, working consent controls and the required certified consent-management setup for applicable regions.

  • Current status: this release does not intentionally load Google AdSense advertising tags or serve AdSense ad units.
  • Future advertising: third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies if advertising is added later. The policy and consent choices must be updated before such deployment.
  • Consent controls: visitors can choose Essential Only or Accept All. Essential Only keeps analytics and advertising consent denied. Accept All enables the analytics consent state; the current release has no AdSense ad units to activate.
  • Regional CMP requirements: before serving ads to users in regions where Google requires a certified CMP and IAB TCF integration, the site operator must implement and validate the applicable Google-certified CMP. The custom consent banner in this release is not represented as an IAB TCF-certified CMP.
  • Preferences: you can reopen the consent banner using the Cookie Preferences link in the site footer.

5. Analytics & Hosting Logs

Google Analytics 4 is configured for the site, but the Google tag is requested from Google's servers only after a visitor explicitly selects Accept All or has previously saved that choice. Before consent, the page sets analytics and advertising consent to denied and does not load the Google Analytics script. Selecting Essential Only keeps that script disabled.

After consent, Google Analytics may receive page-view and technical usage information such as the page visited, browser/device details, approximate location and interaction with the site. The current site code does not intentionally send test answers or individual test scores as Google Analytics events. See Google's privacy information for how Google handles data received through its services.

The hosting provider may also generate ordinary server access and security logs, which can include IP address, requested path, timestamp, user-agent and referrer information. The archive does not specify the hosting provider's log-retention period or configuration; the site operator should confirm those details with the host rather than assume a particular retention or anonymisation setting.

6. Children & Student Privacy

Students can use the practice tests without creating an account or providing a name, phone number or email address. Practice preferences, bookmarks and attempt summaries are stored locally in the browser as described above.

The Contact Desk and question-discrepancy feature allow a visitor to prepare an email draft and may include a name, email address or message supplied by that visitor. Those details are not saved by MockTestVault to browser local storage in this release. Students under the age applicable in their jurisdiction should ask a parent or guardian to handle contact submissions and should not include sensitive personal information in a report.

This operational description is not a legal certification of compliance with COPPA or any other child-privacy law. The site operator should obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before directing services or advertising to children.

7. Privacy Choices & Requests

  • Browser data: you can remove local preferences, bookmarks and attempt summaries by clearing site data for mocktestvault.com in your browser.
  • Analytics choice: use Cookie Preferences to reopen the banner. Essential Only keeps Google Analytics disabled. If you previously accepted, choosing Essential Only updates the consent state and prevents the tag from loading on subsequent page visits.
  • Email reports: when you send a report through your email application, the message is handled by your email provider and the recipient. Contact the privacy desk for requests concerning copies held by the site operator.
  • Third-party services: when analytics is enabled, Google processes information under its own terms and privacy disclosures. You can also review Google's privacy and advertising controls directly.

Privacy rights and requirements differ by jurisdiction. This notice describes the current site implementation and is not a legal opinion or a guarantee that a particular regulatory regime has been satisfied.
