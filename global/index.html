The IELTS Academic Reading test contains 40 questions (1 mark each). Raw scores are converted to the standard 9-band scale: 39–40 correct corresponds to Band 9.0; 37–38 to Band 8.5; 35–36 to Band 8.0; 33–34 to Band 7.5; 30–32 to Band 7.0; and 27–29 to Band 6.5.
