Common Questions About Global Exams

Key details on IELTS Band scoring, TOEFL iBT timing, GRE General structure, and free international access.

The IELTS Academic Reading test contains 40 questions (1 mark each). Raw scores are converted to the standard 9-band scale: 39–40 correct corresponds to Band 9.0; 37–38 to Band 8.5; 35–36 to Band 8.0; 33–34 to Band 7.5; 30–32 to Band 7.0; and 27–29 to Band 6.5. MockTestVault simulators calculate your exact raw score and estimated Band equivalent instantly upon submission.

MockTestVault's two independent TOEFL academic-reading drills contain 20 passage-based questions each and use a 35-minute practice timer. They focus on passage comprehension, vocabulary in context and inference. They are not adaptive TOEFL iBT 2026 simulations, and their results are not converted to ETS scaled scores. Read the TOEFL iBT 2026 format guide for the current ETS structure.

The shortened GRE General Test features two 12-question Verbal Reasoning sections and two 12-question Quantitative Reasoning sections. MockTestVault covers high-frequency vocabulary in context, reading comprehension arguments, quantitative comparison, and data interpretation with step-by-step mathematical proofs.

The GMAT Focus Edition consists of three 45-minute sections: Quantitative Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning, and Data Insights. MockTestVault focuses heavily on Data Insights—including Data Sufficiency, Table Analysis, and Multi-Source Reasoning using configurable practice timing.
