- Mathematics Standard (041): Remembering/Understanding 54%, Applying 24%, Analysing/Evaluating/Creating 22%.

The 840-question bank is not currently identified as exclusively Basic or Standard, and it contains 20 questions per set per chapter rather than an 80-mark exam blueprint. Therefore these weights are reference benchmarks, not pass/fail criteria for this bank.

IMPORTANT SCOPE DETAILS TO ADJUDICATE
- Triangles: CBSE's 2026-27 content list foregrounds similarity and BPT/converse; additional theorems need review.
- Coordinate Geometry: listed content names distance and internal section formula; extended coordinate constructions need review.
- Trigonometry: only simple identities are specified; advanced identities and range/monotonicity items need review.
- Heights and Distances: the official limit is no more than two right triangles and angles 30°, 45°, 60°.
- Circles: the named theorems are tangent-radius perpendicularity and equal tangents from an external point.
- Areas Related to Circles: segment-area central angles are restricted to 60°, 90°, 120°.
- Surface Areas and Volumes: combinations are of any two of the listed solids.
- Statistics: grouped-data mean, median and mode.
- Probability: classical definition and simple event problems.
