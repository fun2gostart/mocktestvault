PHASE 5.15 DECISION GUIDE
CORE_ALIGNED
The question directly tests prescribed Class X 2026-27 content or a straightforward application/consequence of a named theorem/formula. Retain in core bank, subject to answer/explanation verification.

ENRICHMENT_OPTIONAL
The question uses mathematically valid but not expressly named content, an extended theorem/construction, a more advanced identity, or modelling beyond the stated core boundary. Retain in a separate enrichment/challenge area; do not count it as core syllabus coverage.

OUTSIDE_CORE_SCOPE
Use only where the stem clearly tests content outside the intended course and cannot reasonably be treated as a simple application. No items were conclusively placed in this category in Phase 5.15.
