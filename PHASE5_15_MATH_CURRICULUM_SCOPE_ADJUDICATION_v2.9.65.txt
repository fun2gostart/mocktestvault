IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION

The adjudication distinguishes items that directly test named content from items that use additional theorems, constructions or extended modelling. An enrichment classification does not mean the mathematics is incorrect; it means the item should not be counted as core syllabus coverage without explicit course-owner approval.

KEY CURRICULUM BOUNDARIES USED
1. Triangles: the listed Class X content is triangle similarity, similarity criteria, BPT and its converse. Additional theorems such as Apollonius, angle-bisector and altitude-to-hypotenuse are treated as enrichment unless directly reducible to the listed results.
2. Coordinate Geometry: the prescribed named formulae are distance formula and section formula for internal division. Centroid, circumcentre, reflection and area formula extensions are treated as enrichment.
3. Introduction to Trigonometry: acute-angle ratios, standard values 30°, 45°, 60°, ratio relationships, sin²A + cos²A = 1 and simple identities. High-power, monotonicity/range and compound identities are treated as enrichment.
4. Heights and Distances: no more than two right triangles; angles of elevation/depression only 30°, 45°, 60°. Elaborate rate/time models and angular-diameter problems are enrichment; compliant two-triangle contexts remain core-aligned.
5. Circles: the two named theorems are tangent-radius perpendicularity and equality of tangents from an external point. Direct consequences/applications are retained; secant-power and other extended circle results are enrichment.
6. Areas Related to Circles: sector/arc and area/perimeter applications are retained. Segment-area items are core-aligned only when central angle is 60°, 90° or 120°.
7. Surface Areas and Volumes: combinations of any two listed solids are permitted. Recasting/flow contexts are retained where the calculation is reducible to listed solids and no additional solid type is introduced.
8. Statistics: grouped-data mean, median and mode are core. Cumulative frequency as a calculation aid is retained; graphical ogive construction/intersection is enrichment.
9. Probability: simple classical sample-space events are core. Geometric/continuous probability and multi-stage conditional selection are enrichment.
