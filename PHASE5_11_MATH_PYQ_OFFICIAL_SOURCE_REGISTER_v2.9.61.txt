OFFICIAL SOURCE ROUTING — PHASE 5.11
1. CBSE previous-year question paper archive
   https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/question-paper.html
   Use to locate the exact Class X Mathematics Basic/Standard paper for the target year and paper code/set.

2. CBSE marking-scheme archive
   https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/marking-scheme.html
   Use to locate the matching official marking scheme for the same year, subject and paper variant.

3. CBSE sample question paper archive
   https://cbseacademic.nic.in/sqp_archive.html
   This is for official sample papers/specimen papers. It must not be treated as proof that an item appeared in an annual board examination.

Evidence rule:
An archive URL alone does not authenticate an item. Authentication requires the actual paper and matching marking scheme, with question-level comparison and reviewer record.
