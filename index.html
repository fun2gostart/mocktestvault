Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Transparent answers about our platform, methodology, and educational mission.

❓ Is MockTestVault completely free to use?

Yes, absolutely. MockTestVault is built with an open-access educational philosophy: zero paid subscription tiers, zero mandatory paywalls, and non-intrusive academic presentation. All simulators are open to students and aspirants worldwide.

❓ How closely does the SSC CGL practice tool follow the published structure?

The SSC CGL 2026 notification specifies 100 questions across four subjects, 60 minutes overall with 15-minute sectional timers, +2 marks for a correct answer and -0.50 for an incorrect answer. MockTestVault provides an independent practice simulator and does not reproduce official examination software.

❓ Can I practice on my mobile phone?

Yes. The entire platform is built with full-viewport responsive design. While the layout adapts to smaller screens for convenient mobile revision, it also provides an full-screen CBT-style practice mode when practicing on laptops and desktop browsers.

❓ Are the US and Global test tracks live?

Yes! Digital SAT® Math, Digital SAT® Reading & Writing, and Cambridge IELTS® Academic Reading are live right now in our dedicated US Vault and Global Vault with practice timers, Desmos calculator techniques, indicative band-score references and worked explanations; source and review status varies.
