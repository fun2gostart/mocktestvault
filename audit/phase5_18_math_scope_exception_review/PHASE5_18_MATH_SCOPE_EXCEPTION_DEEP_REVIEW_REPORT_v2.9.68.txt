WHY NO AUTOMATIC RECLASSIFICATION
The CBSE syllabus boundary often depends on the actual solution method, diagram constraints, theorem prerequisites and contextual assumptions, not merely the topic label. The prior phase's 200 low-confidence/ambiguous cases and 150 enrichment candidates therefore require a teacher/SME to record the final decision. This phase makes that review operationally actionable rather than converting a heuristic into a false academic sign-off.
