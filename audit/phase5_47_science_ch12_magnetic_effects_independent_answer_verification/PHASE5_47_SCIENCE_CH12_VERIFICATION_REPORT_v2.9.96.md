## Technical references and scope

Core concepts were checked against the NCERT Class X Science treatment of magnetic fields, solenoids, force on current-carrying conductors, motors, electromagnetic induction and domestic circuits. The nominal Indian single-phase supply is 230 V at 50 Hz; this is supported by the BIS *Plugs and Sockets* document and the APERC Draft Supply Code 2024 (230 V phase-to-neutral; 50 Hz). Some older school texts use 220 V as an approximate legacy figure.

This review checks scientific correctness and internal answer/explanation consistency. It does **not** establish that an item is an exact CBSE previous-year question. Topic-level textbook references are not item-level provenance.
