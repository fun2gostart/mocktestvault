### Scope basis
The CBSE Class X 2026–27 Coordinate Geometry unit explicitly specifies the distance formula and section formula for internal division. The provisional core group is limited to direct use of these prescribed formulas, including midpoint, distance from origin/axes, equal-distance calculations and internal division.
The enrichment group contains extensions such as slope-based collinearity, centroid formula, circumcentre, coordinate-area formula, reflections, perpendicular-bisector equations, and coordinate-based classification or construction tasks.
The one held item is `cbse10-math-ch07-s2-003`, which combines the section formula with a linear boundary equation; a teacher should decide whether it is acceptable as an application of internal division.

### Academic quality caveats
A mathematically consistent key does not establish that a question is pedagogically ideal, source-authentic, unambiguous in every diagram/context, or suitable for the official assessment format. No item is released into a Standard or Basic paper-generation pool in this phase.
