## Provisional scope classification

- Core-aligned provisional: 2
- Enrichment optional provisional: 4
- Human scope adjudication required: 0

The 2026–27 CBSE Mathematics curriculum specifies mean, median and mode of grouped data; it specifically describes direct, assumed-mean and step-deviation methods for mean and algebraic methods for median and mode. It does not explicitly list graphical ogive-based median determination or graphical histogram-based mode estimation in the stated competency. Those graphical-method items are therefore retained as optional enrichment, not treated as incorrect mathematics. Class-mark assumption and cumulative-frequency-to-class-frequency conversion are retained as supporting/core-aligned operations for grouped-data calculations.
