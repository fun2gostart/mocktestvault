CORRECTION
Question ID: cbse10-math-ch01-s2-011 (Set 2, Q11).
The original asked for the value of n where a=2^3*3, b=2*3*5, c=3^n*5 and LCM=2^3*3^2*5. The LCM condition is max(1,1,n)=2, which allows n=1 and n=2. Thus the original stem had two valid choices (1 and 2). The stem now asks for the greatest possible positive integer n. The unique answer is n=2 (option C); answer index is unchanged.
