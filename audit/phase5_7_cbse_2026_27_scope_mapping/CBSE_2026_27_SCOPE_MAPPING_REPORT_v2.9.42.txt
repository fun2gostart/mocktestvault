IMPORTANT SCOPE RULES:
- Board theory: only questions mapped to the Board-assessed theoretical syllabus.
- Map-only Board assessment: Geography – Lifelines of National Economy. Its theory MCQs are not Board-theory questions.
- Periodic Assessment only: History – The Age of Industrialisation.
- The Making of a Global World: Board theory limited to subtopics 1–1.3; later sections mapped to interdisciplinary project/multiple assessment.
- Globalisation and the Indian Economy: Board theory limited to “What is Globalisation?” and “Factors that have enabled Globalisation”; production across countries, Chinese toys, WTO and fair-globalisation content mapped to interdisciplinary project/multiple assessment.
- Other chapters in the prescribed Board theory syllabus are tagged Board theory. Separately prescribed map skills are noted and are not falsely represented as map-pointing questions.

QUALITY / GOVERNANCE LIMITATIONS:
- Mapping is an automated classification based on chapter, question stem and subtopic; it is not academic SME sign-off.
- Borderline questions in split-scope chapters require human review. In particular, the exact subtopic boundary for Globalisation must be checked against the official curriculum PDF before publishing Board-eligibility claims.
- No question text, answer option, answer index or explanation was changed in this phase; only a separate scope-mapping metadata object was added to active question records.
- Existing audit metadata and prior labels were preserved. The baseline contained 180 prior labels and 1,080 missing labels; the new CBSE 2026–27 mapping is additional and does not overwrite those earlier labels.
- No production deployment was performed. AdSense remains out of scope.
