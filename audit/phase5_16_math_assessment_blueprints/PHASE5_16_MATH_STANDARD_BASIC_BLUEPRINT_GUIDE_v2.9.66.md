# Phase 5.16 — Class X Mathematics Standard and Basic Assessment Blueprints

Revision: v2.9.66  
Baseline: v2.9.65  
Status: Draft design reference; not an approved CBSE paper or a production change.

## Purpose
Create separate 80-mark theory-paper blueprint references for Mathematics Standard (041) and Mathematics Basic (241), preserving the official CBSE 2026–27 unit-wise marks and cognitive-typology weightages. The files are design constraints for future paper assembly, not generated examination papers.

## Official blueprint constraints (CBSE 2026–27)

| Unit | Chapters mapped | Marks |
|---|---:|---:|
| Number Systems | 1 | 6 |
| Algebra | 2–5 | 20 |
| Coordinate Geometry | 7 | 6 |
| Geometry | 6, 10 | 15 |
| Trigonometry | 8–9 | 12 |
| Mensuration | 11–12 | 10 |
| Statistics and Probability | 13–14 | 11 |
| **Total** | **1–14** | **80** |

| Typology | Standard (041) | Basic (241) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Remembering + Understanding | 43 marks (54%) | 60 marks (75%) |
| Applying | 19 marks (24%) | 12 marks (15%) |
| Analysing + Evaluating + Creating | 18 marks (22%) | 8 marks (10%) |
| **Total** | **80** | **80** |

The unit distribution and cognitive-typology distribution are separate official constraints. CBSE does not provide a unit-by-typology cross-tab in the cited curriculum table. This phase therefore does not fabricate an official cross-tab or impose an unapproved one.

## Chapter-to-unit mapping used in this draft
- Unit I Number Systems: Chapter 1 Real Numbers.
- Unit II Algebra: Chapters 2 Polynomials, 3 Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables, 4 Quadratic Equations, 5 Arithmetic Progressions.
- Unit III Coordinate Geometry: Chapter 7 Coordinate Geometry.
- Unit IV Geometry: Chapter 6 Triangles and Chapter 10 Circles.
- Unit V Trigonometry: Chapter 8 Introduction to Trigonometry and Chapter 9 Some Applications of Trigonometry.
- Unit VI Mensuration: Chapter 11 Areas Related to Circles and Chapter 12 Surface Areas and Volumes.
- Unit VII Statistics and Probability: Chapter 13 Statistics and Chapter 14 Probability.

This mapping is an operational chapter grouping for the MockTestVault bank and should receive academic owner confirmation before being used to assemble live papers.

## Bank inventory and readiness
- 14 chapters; 3 sets per chapter; 20 questions per set.
- 42 mapped active sets and 840 question IDs in the Phase 5.14 item register.
- Phase 5.15 examined 140 flagged scope candidates: 77 were provisionally marked CORE_ALIGNED and 63 ENRICHMENT_OPTIONAL; all 140 still require human sign-off.
- The other 700 questions were not individually scope-adjudicated in Phase 5.15. They must not be presumed core-aligned solely because they were not flagged.
- Cognitive demand labels L1/L2/L3 are AI-assisted draft labels (45/744/51 across the bank), not teacher-validated ratings and not equivalent to CBSE typology percentages. A question-level re-rating is required before automated paper composition.
- Existing Easy/Medium/Hard labels are not a substitute for cognitive demand or syllabus eligibility.

## Paper assembly rules recommended for the next implementation stage
1. Filter out enrichment items from core-paper assembly until an academic reviewer explicitly approves their placement.
2. Do not treat the 77 provisional core-aligned decisions as approved; human sign-off is pending.
3. Complete scope review for the 700 unadjudicated items before declaring complete core coverage.
4. Re-rate all items against explicit R/U, Applying, and A/E/C definitions with a human reviewer; do not translate L1/L2/L3 mechanically.
5. Assemble question formats and mark splits only after a separate format/section design is approved. This phase does not prescribe MCQ, short-answer, long-answer, case-based or internal-choice counts.
6. Validate each assembled paper against both marginal constraints (unit marks and typology marks), total 80, chapter coverage, no duplicate IDs, answer-key integrity, and syllabus eligibility.
7. Keep Standard and Basic pools distinct at paper-generation time. Do not assume that the same item is suitable for both without a reviewer rating.

## Limitations and sign-off
This is a planning blueprint, not a CBSE sample paper, marking scheme, or guarantee of exact official examination structure. The source syllabus states the marks and typology weights but does not specify a complete unit-by-cognitive matrix in the sections used here. Chapter mapping and bank readiness labels are draft operational aids. Academic sign-off remains pending.
