## Main issue classes corrected

1. Persistent pollutants: removed absolute claims that chemicals such as DDT or methylmercury can never be metabolised or excreted.
2. Ozone chemistry: clarified CFC applications and the role of polar stratospheric clouds in activating chlorine chemistry.
3. Biodegradation: avoided universal claims about all plastics and all microorganisms; corrected cellulase substrate specificity.
4. Waste management: distinguished yellow-category biomedical waste from used sharps, which have a separate white category and treatment route.
5. Ecosystem energy: clarified that energy not transferred to the next trophic level is not all simply lost as heat; uneaten biomass and waste also matter.
6. Food webs and aquatic pollution: removed unsupported universal microplastic biomagnification and overgeneralised biodiversity claims.
7. Ecological/medical precision: corrected diclofenac-vulture mechanism, cataract anatomy, and thermal pollution explanation.
8. Quantitative claims: updated methane global-warming-potential wording and biogas methane proportion to ranges rather than outdated or fixed values.
