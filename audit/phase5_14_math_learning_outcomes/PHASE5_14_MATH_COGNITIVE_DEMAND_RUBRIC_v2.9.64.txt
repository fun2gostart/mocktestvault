COGNITIVE DEMAND RUBRIC — DRAFT FOR TEACHER CONFIRMATION
This L1/L2/L3 scheme is an internal operational rubric, not official CBSE terminology.

L1 — Remember / Understand:
- Recall a definition, theorem statement, formula, standard value or direct conceptual relationship.
- Recognise, classify, interpret a familiar representation, or make a direct one-step inference.

L2 — Apply:
- Apply a known method or formula to a routine numerical or contextual task.
- Execute a standard calculation or familiar procedure, generally with one or more routine steps.

L3 — Analyse / Evaluate / Create:
- Analyse a non-routine situation, connect multiple conditions, compare strategies, infer constraints, justify a conclusion, prove a result, or solve an unfamiliar multi-stage problem.

Do not assign L3 merely because a stem is long, uses a real-world setting, or is labelled "case study". Conversely, a short parameter or proof question may require L3 reasoning.
The question-level proposed levels in this phase are screening suggestions only. They are not a validated item-level cognitive rating and must not be used directly to claim compliance with CBSE question-paper typology weights.
