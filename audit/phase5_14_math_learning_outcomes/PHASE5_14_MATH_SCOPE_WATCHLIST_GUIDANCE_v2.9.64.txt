Chapter-specific checks:
6. Triangles — The syllabus explicitly foregrounds similarity criteria and BPT/converse. Review Pythagoras/altitude, angle-bisector, Apollonius, polygon-similarity and other additional results.
7. Coordinate Geometry — The listed Class X content names distance formula and section formula (internal division). Review centroid, circumcentre, area-of-triangle, perpendicular-bisector, reflection and other constructions.
8. Introduction to Trigonometry — The syllabus includes acute-angle ratios, standard angles, and simple identities based on sin²A + cos²A = 1. Review monotonicity, double/triple-angle, high-power identities, secant/cosecant/cotangent range and advanced transformations.
9. Some Applications of Trigonometry — The syllabus limits heights-and-distances questions to simple problems, no more than two right triangles, and angles 30°, 45° and 60°. Review speed/rate, angular-diameter and multi-stage/complex observation scenarios.
10. Circles — The listed theorems are tangent-radius perpendicularity and equality of tangents from an external point. Review secant relations, chord-of-contact, incircle/circumscribed quadrilaterals and extended tangent-angle properties.
11. Areas Related to Circles — Segment-area questions are restricted to central angles 60°, 90° and 120°. Review other segment angles and extended circle-area constructions.
12. Surface Areas and Volumes — The syllabus specifies combinations of any two of cubes, cuboids, spheres, hemispheres, right circular cylinders and cones. Review multi-solid, unusual recasting and advanced flow/volume scenarios.
13. Statistics — The listed scope is mean, median and mode of grouped data. Review ogives, dispersion, change-of-origin/scale and other extensions.
14. Probability — The stated scope is classical probability and simple event problems. Review geometric probability and complex compound-event / without-replacement problems.
