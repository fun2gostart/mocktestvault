📘 CBSE Class 12 Physics Theory Examination Blueprint (042)

The CBSE Class 12 Physics Theory examination (Subject Code 042) carries 70 marks and tests fundamental conceptual clarity, graphical interpretations, numerical problem-solving, and assertion-reasoning competencies across the NCERT and CBSE curriculum.

⚡ Electrostatics & Magnetism Gauss's law, capacitors with dielectrics, drift velocity, Biot-Savart law, moving coil galvanometers, and magnetic materials.
🔄 EMI & Alternating Current Lenz's law, self/mutual inductance, series LCR resonant circuits, wattless current, and transformer core laminations.
🔍 Optics (Ray & Wave) Lens Maker's formula, total internal reflection, astronomical telescopes, Young's double slit interference, and diffraction fringes.
🔬 Modern Physics & Electronics Photoelectric effect, de Broglie wavelength, Bohr model quantization, binding energy per nucleon, and p-n junction rectifiers.
⚖️ Marking Scheme & Strategy: • Marks per Question: 1 mark per correct answer; zero negative marking in official CBSE multiple choice sections.
• Target Score: 30+/35 marks guarantees an estimated A1 grade trajectory for senior secondary board exams.
• Formula Accuracy: Always verify unit conversions (e.g. converting cm to meters, eV to Joules) before selecting numerical options.
